UC Berkeley MIDS · Whitepaper

Zero-Shot vs. Fine-Tuned: A Systematic Evaluation of Encoder, Decoder, and LLM Architectures for Clinical Text Classification

UC Berkeley MIDS Clinical NLP Transformers LLM Evaluation PubMedBERT Mistral-7B LoRA GPT-4

A systematic comparison of zero-shot LLMs versus fine-tuned transformer architectures for single-label clinical text classification across three real-world, imbalanced healthcare datasets — evaluating encoder-based (PubMedBERT), encoder-decoder (T5-base), and decoder-only LLM (Mistral-7B + LoRA) paradigms against a zero-shot GPT-4 baseline.

Results show domain-specific fine-tuned encoders achieve superior efficiency-performance trade-offs (F1 0.63–0.97), while zero-shot LLMs enable rapid deployment without labeled data (F1 0.48–0.71). Introduces a reproducible zero-shot evaluation framework with optimized system prompts, constraint-based output formatting, and statistical significance testing — directly applicable to enterprise LLM evaluation at scale.
